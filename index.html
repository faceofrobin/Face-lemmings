07 / Typography

Words are places, too.

Not all who wander are lost. Some are circular and perfectly indifferent to the laws of locomotion. The Face has no legs, yet it persists. The sentence is long enough to qualify as infrastructure.

To move is not necessarily to progress. To stop is not necessarily to arrive.
