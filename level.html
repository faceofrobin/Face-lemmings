How to Play

Press Start to release a procession of Faces. They roll automatically over ramps and bridges, collect in bowls, and fall if there is no ground beneath them. Tap each black-and-white gate control to open it; tap a bridge control to extend its missing span. Save the required number at the EXIT. Use the arrows or level selector to visit any of the 16 journeys. Restart begins the current level again. Some obstacles must be cleared before the procession arrives; others may hold the Faces until you're ready to send them onward.
