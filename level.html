01 / OPENING ARGUMENT

Everything begins somewhere.

And here is somewhere. The words are not merely words. They are little ledges that hold up the weight of a very small, very determined Face.

Premise

Movement implies purpose. Purpose implies a destination.

Objection

Neither implication has been established.

02 / THE COMMITTEE

We have formed a committee.

Its task is to determine where everyone is going, why they are going there, and whether or not they should bring a hat.

Minutes were taken. No one remembers by whom.
03 / AN IMAGE APPEARS

The Face encounters itself.

This is an image. It is also a platform, an obstacle, and possibly a mirror. The Face looks back at the Face. Nothing is clarified.

04 / THREE DEPARTMENTS

One office. Several opinions.

Intake

Records the arrival of ideas.

Transit

Moves them to another desk.

Archives

Forgets where the desks are.

05 / THE LONG SENTENCE

A digression.

There is a particular kind of journey that begins in the middle of a sentence and continues for as long as it takes the speaker to arrive at a point that may or may not have existed at the beginning, with several commas, more than one qualification, and an undeniable sense of ceremonial progress.

Then, unexpectedly, the sentence ends.

06 / TWO CHOICES

The illusion of control.

Choose your preferred interpretation of what is happening here. The Faces will not be consulted.

07 / AN EXHIBITION

Several objects of no known origin.

Three distinct works, all entirely indistinguishable. The curatorial explanation has been mislaid.

Dear Recipient,

We are writing to inform you that progress has been made in an unspecified direction.

Please accept this update in the spirit with which it was intended, whatever that may have been.

12 / ANOTHER IMAGE

Evidence of repetition.

To encounter The Face once is coincidence. To encounter it twice is an invitation to reconsider coincidence.

13 / FORMALITIES

Please sign here.

There is nothing to agree to. There are no terms. There is only a box that resembles a form.

14 / THE FINAL MEETING

A resolution is proposed.

Resolved, that the journey shall continue until it stops.

The resolution passes unanimously, although the vote is not recorded.

15 / THE APPROACH

We may be nearly there.

Looking back

Many things have happened.

Looking ahead

There appears to be a bottom.

16 / THE LAST PAGE

A conclusion refuses to appear.

There will be a finish line shortly. Whether that represents triumph, failure, or an administrative convenience has not yet been determined.

Nevertheless, the Face proceeds.
